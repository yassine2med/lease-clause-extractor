OFFICE LEASE, SUITE 100, BUILDING A

1. Term. The lease shall commence on March 1, 2024 and expire on February 28, 2029.
2. Rent. Tenant shall pay base rent of $12,500.00 per month, due on the first day of each month.
3. Increases. Base rent will increase by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
4. Security Deposit. Tenant has paid a security deposit of $25,000.00 to Landlord.
5. Renewal. Tenant may renew for one term of five years by giving written notice at least 180 days before expiration.
6. Early Termination. Either party may terminate this lease after year three with ninety (90) days written notice.
